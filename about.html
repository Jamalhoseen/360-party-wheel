From a Garage to a Full-Circle Rental Company

We started with a stack of folding chairs and a borrowed truck. Eight years later, we're the crew San Diego calls when an event has to go right.

Our Story

Built for One Quinceañera. Kept Going for 2,500 Events.

360 Party Wheel started in 2018 when our founder couldn't find a photo booth vendor in San Diego who could deliver the spinning, slow-motion experience his sister wanted for her quinceañera. So he built one himself — welded frame, borrowed camera, a lot of trial and error.

The booth was a hit. Requests started coming in for the booth, then for chairs to go with it, then tables, then a tent. Within two years we'd outgrown the garage and moved into a warehouse in Chula Vista.

Today we stock over 15,000 rental items and deliver across all of San Diego County — but the name stuck, because the idea never changed: cover every angle of the event, so you don't have to.

The Journey

Eight Years, One Mission

We've grown a lot since that first quinceañera — but every milestone still traces back to the same goal: fewer headaches for the host, better memories for the guests.
